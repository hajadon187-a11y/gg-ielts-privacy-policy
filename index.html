English

1. Operator and contact

This app, “Gachi Guild Flashcard for TOEFL (GG TOEFL),” is provided by Gachi Guild (“the Operator”).

Contact: gachiguild@gmail.com

2. Data handled by the app

The app is designed primarily for on-device learning. It does not require an account and does not send learning data to a server operated by the Operator. The Android app does not request the INTERNET permission.

  • Vocabulary mastery status, favorites, and study history
  • Daily goals, study streaks, reminder settings, language, accent, theme, and other app settings

The Operator does not use advertising, analytics, or behavioral-tracking SDKs in this app.

3. Notifications

If notifications are allowed and an active study streak is at risk, the app may show a reminder at approximately 9:00 p.m. local time when the daily study goal has not been completed. Notification content is generated on the device and is not sent to the Operator. The app may reschedule this reminder after device restart or time-zone changes.

4. ChatGPT and Gemini

For optional AI conversation practice, the app may check on the device whether the supported ChatGPT or Gemini app is installed and may open that app at the user’s request. The result is not sent to the Operator.

The app does not automatically send vocabulary, study history, or other learning data to those services. Information entered into an external AI app is handled under that service’s privacy policy and terms.

5. Text-to-speech

The app uses the Android device’s Text-to-Speech engine for words, meanings, collocations, and example sentences. Depending on the installed TTS engine, voice data may be downloaded or network communication may occur under that provider’s policy. The app does not send TTS text to the Operator.

6. Google Play purchases

Google Play Billing is used for premium features. Google Play processes payment and card information. The Operator does not receive card numbers or other payment details, but receives information necessary to verify purchases and unlock features.

7. Backup and restore

When the user explicitly creates a .toeflg-backup file, the app writes vocabulary data, translations, pronunciation information, collocations, examples, study history, favorites, goals, streaks, and display settings to a destination selected by the user.

Backup files are GZIP-compressed but are not encrypted by the app. Users should review the security and privacy practices of any storage or cloud service they choose. Restore occurs only after the user selects a backup file and confirms the restore operation.

8. Sharing, retention, and deletion

The Operator does not sell or provide learning data to advertising or analytics services. Learning data remains on the device until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Backup files must be deleted by the user from their storage location.

The app has no Operator-managed account or server-side user data, so there is no separate account-deletion procedure. To delete local data, uninstall the app or clear its app data in Android settings.

9. Security and changes

Data is stored using Android app storage and device mechanisms. The Operator cannot eliminate all risks from device loss, rooting, malicious apps, or sharing backup files.

This policy may be updated when laws, Google Play requirements, or app features change. The updated date will be changed when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or other inquiries, contact gachiguild@gmail.com. Because learning data is stored locally, the Operator cannot remotely delete data from the user’s device.

2. Datos tratados

La aplicación funciona principalmente en el dispositivo, no requiere una cuenta y no envía los datos de estudio a un servidor del Operador. Guarda el dominio de vocabulario, favoritos, historial, objetivos diarios, rachas, recordatorios y ajustes. No utiliza SDK de publicidad, analítica ni seguimiento de comportamiento.

3. ChatGPT, Gemini y TTS

Para ofrecer orientación opcional de conversación con IA, la aplicación puede comprobar en el dispositivo si están instaladas aplicaciones compatibles de ChatGPT o Gemini. El resultado no se envía al Operador. La aplicación no envía automáticamente datos de estudio a una IA externa. La lectura usa el motor TTS de Android y la política del proveedor correspondiente.

4. Google Play, copias de seguridad y eliminación

Las funciones premium utilizan Google Play Billing y Google Play procesa la información de pago. Las copias creadas por el usuario pueden contener vocabulario, historial, favoritos, objetivos, rachas y ajustes. Se comprimen con GZIP, pero la aplicación no las cifra. Los datos permanecen en el dispositivo hasta desinstalar la aplicación o borrar sus datos; el usuario debe eliminar las copias de seguridad.

Los datos se guardan mediante el almacenamiento de Android, pero no se pueden eliminar por completo los riesgos de pérdida del dispositivo, root, aplicaciones maliciosas o compartir copias.
